# Trademarks

[Português](docs/pt-BR/TRADEMARKS.md) · [Español](docs/es/TRADEMARKS.md)

The code is licensed under [Apache 2.0](LICENSE). The name "refy" and the refy logo are not: section 6 of the license keeps them out.

## You can

- Say that your product is built with refy, or uses refy.
- Use the name to refer to the project in articles, talks, courses and comparisons.
- Keep the name in an unmodified copy of the packages.

## You cannot

- Name your product, package, theme or company "refy", or a name that can be confused with it.
- Publish a modified version as "refy", or suggest that the refy project made or endorses it.
- Use the logo as the logo of your product.

A fork must use another name. Questions go to an [issue](https://github.com/viniciuscoimbra/refy-design/issues).
